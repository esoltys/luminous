Basics

Getting started

Luminous plays the music already on your computer. You don't need a library to start.

Play something now

•
Under Play music, choose Open Files or Play a Folder.
•
Or drop files and folders onto the window.

Build a library

1
Choose Add Folder to Library under Build a library, or add a folder in Settings → Sources.
2
Luminous scans it, then keeps watching. New, moved and renamed files update on their own.
3
Browse your music under Collection in the sidebar.

Home

•
Home shows a running snapshot of your library and listening.
•
Top Albums ranks albums by minutes played this week. A Re-entry badge marks an album back on the chart.
•
Set which day the week starts with Start the week with in Settings → General.

Take the tour

•
New to Luminous? A short walkthrough introduces the interface.
•
Replay it anytime with Restart Feature Walkthrough in Help.
Basics

Your collection

Collection in the sidebar has four views: Artists, Albums, Songs and Genres. Each shows a count and a sort control at the top right.

Songs

•
See your whole library as a sortable table.
•
Right-click any column header to choose the fields shown, such as Play Count and Skip Count.
•
Drag a column header's right edge to resize the column. Double-click the edge to reset its width.

Genres

•
Each genre is a card. Its song count includes its sub-genres.
•
Drag a sub-genre chip onto another card to move it, or drag a card's handle to make the whole genre a sub-genre.
•
Right-click a chip or card header to Rename, Promote to top-level genre or Delete it from every song.
•
Click a card's colour swatch to change its colour.
•
Click a card or chip to open its auto playlist.
•
Tags you add on artist pages get their own cards under Artist Tags, above the song genres.
•
At the top right, switch between card and row views, and sort by name or song count.
•
Songs without a genre are grouped under No Genre.

Albums

•
Open an album to see its tracks. The side panel shows the playing file's format, bitrate, sample rate and loudness.
•
Click + to add the album or a song to the active playlist (the Queue, unless you choose another).
•
Click the pencil to open Edit Album Tags and fix the title, artist, genre or year of every track at once.

Edit tags

•
Click a track's pencil to open Edit Song Tags.
•
Artist, Album Artist, Composer and Genre accept several values. Press Enter after each one.
•
Clear Artwork removes the track's embedded cover.

Artists

•
An artist page lists releases under Discography: Albums, EPs and Singles, then playlists featuring the artist.
•
Click Edit to add a bio, website and links such as Bandcamp, Spotify or Instagram. They appear in the artist's About panel.

Playback

The player bar sits at the bottom of the window while music plays.

Player bar

•
Click the cover art for Immersive Mode (Ctrl2).
•
Click the heart or stars to rate the song. Choose the style in Settings → General.
•
Click the waveform to jump within the song. beside it switches to frequency bands: blue for bass, amber for mids, white for highs.
•
Shuffle cycles through Off, All and:
– Inside Album: albums in order, tracks shuffled within each.
– Albums: albums shuffled, tracks in order.
– Artists: one artist at a time, artists picked at random.
•
Repeat cycles through Off, Song, Album and Playlist.
•
The buttons on the right open:
the song menu
lyrics
the Queue
the info panel (CtrlI)
the mini player (CtrlM)
•
Media keys and your system's media controls work even when Luminous isn't in front.

Mini player

•
Hover over the mini player to show its controls and rate the song.
•
Drag along its top edge to move it.
•
Use the lyrics button to switch between the cover and live lyrics.

Basics

Playlists

Playlists come in three kinds:

•
Custom: build it yourself by dragging songs in.
•
Smart: set rules, such as "Genre is Rock and Year is after 2015", and Luminous keeps it up to date.
•
Auto: Genre, Decade and BPM playlists Luminous builds itself, with up to 25 songs refreshed daily. Each appears once 25 songs match. Click Refresh Playlist for a new pick.

Add songs

•
Click + beside a song or album to add it to the Queue.
•
To add to another playlist instead, click Make Active on it first.
•
Drop files or a folder onto the window to replace the Queue and play. Hold Shift to add them to the end instead.

Smart playlists

•
Click New Smart Playlist, add rules, then name and save it.
•
Use Edit Rules to change them later.

BPM playlists

BPM playlists use each song's BPM tag. Luminous doesn't measure tempo, so songs without a BPM tag aren't included. Make your own ranges with a smart playlist.

Manage a playlist

•
Double-click the name to rename it.
•
Drag rows to reorder. Select several to move them together.
•
Click a column header to sort, or type in the filter box to narrow the list.
•
Use the remove buttons to drop duplicate or unavailable songs, and Clear Playlist to empty it.
•
Right-click a column header to add a Date Added column.

Import & export

•
Import or export .m3u, .m3u8, .pls and .xspf playlists.
•
When exporting, choose Use relative (portable) file paths so the playlist still works in another folder or on another computer.

Advanced

Advanced Search

Type field:value terms in the search bar to filter your whole library. For example, genre:rock rating:>=4 finds rock songs rated 4 stars or more.

Syntax

•
A text field without an operator means contains: genre:rock matches any genre containing "rock".
•
A number field without an operator means exactly: year:1994 matches only 1994.
•
Several terms must all match: rating:>=4 year:<2000 genre:rock.
•
Words without a colon search title, artist and album: rating:>=4 "tom cochrane".
•
Put values with spaces in quotes: artist:"tom cochrane". Field names ignore case.

Settings

General

•
Language / Langue: English or French.
•
Song rating style: a heart, 5 stars, or both.
•
Start the week with: Sunday or Monday, for weekly charts and stats.
•
Minimize to tray: closing the window hides Luminous in the tray. Right-click the tray icon for Play/Pause, Next and Previous.
•
Launch at login: start Luminous when you sign in.
•
Data Storage Location: where your library database, artwork cache and preferences are kept. The Windows ZIP runs in Portable Mode, keeping everything in a data folder beside the app. On Linux, put an empty file named portable beside the AppImage to do the same.
•
Updates: check for a new version and choose what happens when one is out. Microsoft Store installs update themselves.
Settings

Sources

Folders

•
Click Add Folder to add a music folder. New and changed files appear on their own.
•
Turn off Real-Time Folder Watching to check for changes only when you rescan.
•
Rescan Library on Startup checks your folders each time Luminous opens.
•
Rescan Library looks for changes; Force Full Re-Scan rereads everything.
•
Save artwork next to your music saves covers as cover.jpg in album folders and artist photos as artist.jpg. Existing folder art is never overwritten.

Remote music

•
Remote WebDAV: stream from Nextcloud, ownCloud, Synology and other WebDAV servers.
•
Media Servers: connect an OpenSubsonic server such as Navidrome, Gonic or LMS. Luminous syncs its library and can report your plays back.
•
Remote music is read-only: tag edits and artwork changes stay in Luminous.
Settings

Integrations

Each integration is optional. Turn any of them off to keep that part of Luminous offline.

•
Online Data Sources: ratings, tags, reviews and bios from MusicBrainz, CritiqueBrainz and Wikipedia. Turn off Fetch context & bio data to stay offline.
•
ListenBrainz Scrobbler: paste your User Token, click Validate & Save, then turn on scrobbling. Choose whether to also send now-playing status and ratings.
•
Pause all scrobbling stops sending without disconnecting. Listens made offline wait until you click Sync Now.
•
MusicBrainz Picard: Luminous finds Picard or lets you browse to it, so you can open files in Picard.
•
Discord Rich Presence: show what you're playing on Discord. The Discord desktop app must be running.
•
fanart.tv: fetch artist photos and other art Luminous can't find locally. Add your API key and click Validate & Save. Without a key, Luminous uses Wikidata images.

Settings

Equalizer

Find it under Settings → Equalizer. Every change applies instantly, even mid-song.

10-band

•
Turn on Enable EQ, then pick a preset or shape your own curve.
•
Drag a slider to boost or cut its frequency, up to ±20 dB.
•
Lower the Preamp when you boost, so loud passages don't clip.
•
10-band and Parametric keep separate bands, preamp and preset, so switching never changes the other.

Parametric

•
Drag a node to set its frequency and gain. Scroll on it to make it wider or narrower (Q).
•
Double-click the graph to add a band, up to 20. The table below lists each band's type, values and on/off switch.
•
Reset Bands returns to flat.
•
The ⋯ menu beside the preset holds the preset actions:
– Import… loads a headphone correction profile (see below).
– Export… saves the bands and preamp as an Equalizer APO file.
– Save as… keeps the current bands as your own preset, under My presets.
– Rename and Delete manage your own presets.

Correct your headphones

•
Find your headphones on AutoEq, choose EqualizerAPO ParametricEq as the equalizer app, and download the settings file.
•
In Parametric, choose Import… from the ⋯ menu, then Choose file… or paste the text.
•
The profile is saved under My presets and applied right away. If Luminous can't reproduce it exactly, it names the problem line and changes nothing.

Loudness Normalization

•
Turn it on to even out the volume between songs.
•
Target Level sets how loud everything plays.
•
Normalize by Album to keep the volume differences between songs on the same album.
•
Songs not analyzed yet use their ReplayGain tags, or the Fallback Gain.

Playback Fades & Crossfade

•
Fade on Pause/Stop ramps the volume down instead of cutting it.
•
Manual Song Crossfade blends songs when you skip; Automatic Song Crossfade blends them as a song ends.
•
Preserve gapless for same album keeps live and concept albums seamless.
