# v3.0.0

Luminous 3.0 speaks seven languages, lets you love a song and rate it at the same time, adds a free-frequency parametric EQ with AutoEq headphone profiles, brings live lyrics to the mini player, and keeps your library organized as it grows. It also adds a context-aware info sidebar, pinned items in the sidebar, MusicBrainz sign-in, portable mode, and much faster large WebDAV libraries.

### 🌍 Seven Languages (Epic [#1449](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1449))
- **New UI languages**: German ([#1456](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1456)), Spanish ([#1455](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1455)), Italian ([#1421](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1421)), Russian ([#1458](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1458)) and Ukrainian ([#1457](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1457)) join English and French, along with regional English (US, UK) and French (France) variants ([#1460](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1460), [#1459](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1459)).
- **Translated user guide** ([#1521](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1521)): the guide is rewritten as short sections ([#1404](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1404)) and available in every UI language, with its own language setting on the new System tab ([#1451](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1451)).
- **Everything localized**: the welcome screen and legal documents ([#1612](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1612)), the tray menu, taskbar buttons and update notification ([#1592](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1592), [#1597](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1597)), correct plurals ([#1472](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1472)), locale-aware decimals ([#1342](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1342)), and Wikipedia bios and CritiqueBrainz reviews in your language where available ([#1480](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1480)).
- **New body font**: Luminous now bundles Fira Sans, including Cyrillic ([#1339](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1339), [#1453](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1453)).

### ❤️ Love and Rate (Epic [#1275](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1275))
- **Heart and stars together** ([#1384](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1384), [#1385](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1385)): love a song, dislike it with a right-click on the heart, and give it 1 to 5 stars independently. Choose whether to show the heart, the stars or both.
- **MusicBrainz sign-in** ([#1388](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1388), [#1408](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1408)): log in with your MusicBrainz account to sync loves and ratings both ways with ListenBrainz and CritiqueBrainz ([#1386](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1386)).
- **Album ratings and reviews**: Album Detail falls back to the MusicBrainz community rating ([#1571](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1571)) and links to Review on CritiqueBrainz ([#1387](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1387)).

### 🎚️ Parametric EQ (Epic [#1331](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1331))
- **Free-frequency bands** ([#1332](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1332), [#1333](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1333), [#1334](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1334)): up to 20 bands with bell or shelf types. Drag them on the response graph or type exact values.
- **AutoEq import** ([#1336](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1336), [#1410](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1410)): load a headphone correction profile from AutoEq.
- **Presets** ([#1335](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1335), [#1344](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1344)): save your own, and see a response-curve preview beside each preset name.

### 🎤 Lyrics (Epic [#1294](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1294))
- **Live lyrics in the mini player** ([#1365](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1365)).
- **Word-by-word sync, timing offset and right-to-left text**, plus a new lyrics source ([#1237](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1237)).
- **.srt and .vtt sidecar lyric files** ([#1190](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1190)).

### 🧭 Navigation and Info Sidebar
- **Info sidebar** ([#1622](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1622), [#1630](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1630)): the right panel follows what you're looking at, showing the selected song or the album or artist you're viewing. Unpin it into a hover overlay ([#1657](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1657)), which replaces the Artist and Album Info buttons, or close it from its new close box ([#1641](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1641)).
- **Pinned items in the sidebar** (Epic [#1567](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1567)): playlists, albums and artists pinned to Home also appear in the sidebar. Drag them to reorder, or click one and press Alt+↑ or Alt+↓.
- **Grids instead of carousels** ([#1616](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1616)), and a Cards/Rows toggle for Pinned and the Artist Detail sections ([#1620](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1620)). Recently Added now comes before Top Albums on Home.
- **Search highlights the song** you picked when it opens the album ([#1280](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1280)).

### 📁 Library and Organizing
- **Auto-organize** ([#1372](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1372), [#1468](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1468), [#1469](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1469)): new files and tag changes are moved into your folder pattern as they arrive. Template presets keep optional parts in braces.
- **Portable mode** ([#1273](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1273)): keep app data next to the executable, with a standalone Windows ZIP. Library folders re-link automatically when a drive letter changes ([#1403](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1403)).
- **Large libraries and WebDAV** (Epic [#1481](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1481)): much faster first and repeat WebDAV syncs ([#1483](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1483), [#1493](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1493), [#1509](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1509), [#1514](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1514)), detection of files removed from the server ([#1494](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1494)), a report of files that failed to sync and why ([#1495](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1495), [#1503](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1503)), and less memory used by the Albums grid ([#1528](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1528)).
- **Scan progress** names the library and the current phase ([#1308](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1308)).
- **Auto-Sync on by default** for new Media Servers and WebDAV servers ([#1205](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1205)). The Folders settings tab is now called Sources.
- **Genre and artist-tag hierarchy** travel with a Default Library as a JSON sidecar ([#1312](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1312)), and bio sidecars hold Markdown only, edited in a shared Markdown editor ([#1313](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1313)).

### 🖼️ Artwork and Artists
- **fanart.tv** artist logos and backgrounds, album covers and disc art, each with its own retrieval setting ([#1276](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1276), [#1277](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1277)).
- **Opt-in sidecar artwork**: save covers and artist images into your music folders ([#1274](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1274)).
- **Folder art first**: folder art is preferred over embedded art, embedded art is extracted once per album ([#1278](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1278)), and cached covers are downscaled ([#1272](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1272)).
- **Upcoming concerts** from MusicBrainz on Artist Detail ([#1431](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1431)).
- **Missing metadata guidance**: an empty enrichment panel now points to MusicBrainz and Picard ([#1667](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1667)).

### ▶️ Playback
- **Auto Continue** ([#1235](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1235)): keep playing similar music when the queue runs out.
- **Session wrap** in the mini player and Immersive View when a queue finishes ([#1379](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1379), [#1380](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1380)), and a queue position indicator in the mini player ([#1605](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1605)).
- **Smart playlist dates** ([#1681](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1681)): pick a date or a relative window for Last Played and Date Added. Moment Mix headers show what each mix is built from ([#1682](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1682)).
- **Add to a specific playlist** straight from the song menu ([#1270](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1270)), and exclude a whole album from stats ([#1252](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1252)).

### ⚡ Under the Hood & Architecture
- **Performance**: Tokio stalls are attributed in the log and the ones found are fixed ([#1291](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1291), [#1671](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1671)), large libraries got a broad speed-up ([#1439](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1439)), and memory is measured against a real library in an isolated profile ([#1197](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1197)). See [docs/PERFORMANCE.md](../PERFORMANCE.md) for the 2.0 to 3.0 trend.
- **Exe size** ([#1647](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1647)): user guide screenshots are embedded as WebP, keeping the build small despite seven guides.
- **Architecture lint** ([#1239](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1239)): ast-grep rules check the AGENTS.md invariants in CI, alongside locale completeness ([#1240](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1240)) and Tauri version-mismatch gates ([#1208](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1208)).
- **Real-app screenshots** (Epic [#1557](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1557)): a scene runner drives a throwaway-profile build to capture user guide and Store screenshots.
- **Responsive layout**: named breakpoints shared by CSS and JS ([#1154](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1154)), with content views on container queries ([#1466](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1466)).
- **SvelteKit 3** ([#1550](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1550)), deterministic tests with mocked clocks ([#1296](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1296), [#1323](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1323)), and packaging fixes for Flatpak and AppStream ([#1662](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1662), [#1664](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1664), [#1666](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1666), [#1668](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1668)).
