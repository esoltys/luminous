# v2.0.0

Luminous 2.0. The headline additions are a full Personal Stats view, richer artist/album context (bios, portraits, MusicBrainz/CritiqueBrainz/Wikipedia data), a revamped Organize workflow, and the ability to pin anything to Home — alongside a big batch of visual polish, performance work, and packaging fixes across Windows and Linux.

### 📊 Personal Stats
- **New Stats view** ([#130](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/130)): total minutes listened, a range-aware Listening Streak card, an illustrated Time of Day graphic, and Top Artists/Top Albums lists, switchable between the past 7 days, 28 days, and year.
- **Listening Streak** replaces the earlier daily heatmap with a clearer, range-aware current/longest-streak card.
- A **weekly Top Albums chart** now appears on Home, linked through to the full Stats view.

### 🏠 Home & Pinning
- **Pin to Home** ([#222](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/222)): pin songs, albums, artists, playlists, and auto-playlists to a dedicated row on Home, including the smart "Moment Mix" and "Missing Metadata" auto-playlists ([#667](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/667)).
- **Moment Mix**: a new time-of-day auto-playlist that refreshes through the day.

### 🎨 Artist & Album Context
- **Context & Bio panel** ([#23](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/23)): MusicBrainz ratings and tags, CritiqueBrainz reviews, and a Wikipedia summary now surface right in the Details pane.
- **Artist portraits, band logos, and fanart** with a hierarchical local-artwork scanner ([#757](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/757), [#758](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/758), [#761](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/761)), plus an Artist Rescan action to clear a stale artwork cache.
- **Markdown bios** for artists and albums, mirrored to `artist.md`/`album.md` sidecar files alongside your music.
- **MusicBrainz ID column**, and a **Missing Metadata** auto-playlist plus a Picard bridge for fixing it in bulk.
- **Album social share cards** for sharing what you're listening to.

### 🗂️ Organize Revamp
- Organize moved out from under Settings into its own top-level sidebar item, with the workflow split into clearer sections (Preview, Template Pattern, Destination & Options) and a live "N unorganized songs" summary.
- Duplicate-file detection fixed and the status column simplified to a single, accurate summary line.

### 🎨 Themes & Appearance
- **Metallic theme refreshed** and **four new predefined themes** added.
- **Explicit Light/Dark/System selector**, and a layered-ellipse ambient background for Immersive view.
- Feature walkthrough tour and a first-run welcome screen for new installs.
- Windows taskbar thumbnail toolbar now shows album art with playback controls.
- Real launch-at-login support, and a "Restart to update" prompt instead of abrupt auto-installs.

### ⚙️ Other Improvements
- **CUE sheet support** for single-file album rips ([#78](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/78)).
- **foo_dr.txt Dynamic Range Meter** logs are now parsed and shown.
- "Not included" flag for songs, to exclude specific tracks from auto-playlists and bulk album/artist play.
- Original Year is now an editable field and table column.
- Crash/panic diagnostics are now captured to a log file you can export ([#684](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/684)).
- Media-key/system-media-control integration rewritten as hand-rolled SMTC (Windows) and MPRIS (Linux) backends, replacing a third-party dependency.

### 🐛 Fixes
- Blank window no longer appears after restoring Luminous from a long minimize on Windows.
- The Queue no longer hides or removes already-played tracks; Previous no longer wraps to the last track at the start of the Queue.
- Fixed a Bluetooth output-device switch that could hang playback.
- "Open in Picard" no longer fails on large or already-running selections.
- Fixed the equalizer's band grid clipping on narrow window widths.
- Fixed Collection view jank via indexed artist lookups and debounced search.
- Fixed inconsistent card heights across mixed Playlist/AutoPlaylist/Artist grids, and Artist card hover state mismatches.
- Fixed the Home greeting/detail header layout and genre chip styling inconsistencies.
- Fixed the "Import" label in the playlist overflow menu reading inconsistently next to "Export Playlist".
- Fixed artist/album overview text wrapping on narrow windows.
- Fixed AppImage/deb/rpm packages on Linux not matching their taskbar icon (`StartupWMClass`).
- Fixed Flatpak-installed Discord and Picard not being detected on Linux.
- Fixed a spurious "songs updated" toast that could fire from a watcher-pause timing race.
- Fixed icon alignment in error/status banners once their text wraps to multiple lines.
- General polish pass across the sidebar, Stats, and Organize views (icon alignment, animated stat bars, sidebar collapse/expand sequencing, wider Organize layout).

### 🛠️ Under the Hood
- Backend console logging is now quiet at steady state, with real warnings/errors still surfaced.
- Enabled LTO in the release build profile.
- `cargo clippy` now runs on every PR with zero warnings enforced.
