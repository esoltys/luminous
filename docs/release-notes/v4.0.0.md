# v4.0.0

Luminous 4.0 is a calmer, simpler app with a much bigger toolbox underneath: sync your library to portable players, browse your files and devices side by side, check where your music came from and whether it is intact, curate and de-duplicate your collection, scrobble to Last.fm, and play at any speed — all in a quieter interface that puts your music first.

### 🌿 A Calmer Interface (Epic [#2038](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/2038))
- **Less chrome, more music** ([#2041](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/2041), [#2043](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/2043), [#2044](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/2044), [#2045](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/2045), [#2050](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/2050)): Sidebar labels lose their counts, Home drops its card boxes, star ratings appear only once something is rated, album cards are simpler, and labels use sentence case instead of tracked capitals.
- **Quieter headers** ([#2119](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/2119), [#2114](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/2114), [#2090](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/2090)): Artist, Album, Playlist and auto-playlist headers share one calm layout, and genre chips are gone from artist cards.
- **Play dock and themes** ([#2042](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/2042), [#2046](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/2046), [#2051](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/2051)): Secondary controls move into the song menu, the fixed themes glow with your artwork's colour under the play dock, and the dynamic theme keeps its surfaces to one hue.
- **Plain language** ([#2048](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/2048), [#2049](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/2049)): Organize presets are described as a folder outline instead of raw patterns, and the Equalizer and loudness settings use everyday wording.
- **A first-run screen built around the Luminous mark** ([#2047](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/2047)), a **right-edge notch** to reopen the info sidebar ([#2123](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/2123)), and **state that never relies on colour alone** — errors and warnings now use theme text colours plus an icon or wording ([#1878](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1878)).

### 🔌 Portable Devices & Files (Epics [#834](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/834), [#1881](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1881))
- **Sync to portable players** ([#835](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/835), [#836](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/836), [#837](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/837), [#838](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/838)): Detect USB players and SD cards, preview exactly what a sync will copy or remove, convert formats for the target hardware, and pick albums, artists or playlists to send ([#1977](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1977)).
- **MTP devices** ([#840](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/840), [#1845](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1845), [#1893](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1893)): Android players work on Windows and on Linux through gvfs and kio-mtp.
- **Fit a playlist to a size** ([#344](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/344)): A visual size limit marker shows what fits on the device.
- **Files view** ([#1841](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1841), [#839](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/839), [#1084](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1084)): Browse folders, media servers and devices in a column browser, with right-click menus, multi-selection, sorting, drag and drop and keyboard navigation ([#1884](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1884), [#1888](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1888)). Jump to a song, album or playlist with **View in Files** ([#1883](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1883)), open sidecar files in their default app ([#1885](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1885)), and hand files to the Tag Editor or MusicBrainz Picard ([#1887](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1887)).
- **Playlists survive missing songs** ([#1870](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1870)): Playlist entries and their metadata are kept when songs leave the library.
- **Opened files stay out of your library** ([#1842](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1842)), with an optional suggestion to add their folder ([#1871](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1871), [#2037](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/2037)).

### 🔍 Provenance, Integrity & Curation (Epics [#1441](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1441), [#1585](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1585))
- **Fake FLAC detection** ([#1373](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1373)): Lossless files are checked for upsampled or transcoded sources.
- **Corrupt file detection** ([#1938](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1938)): Verify audio integrity and flag damaged songs.
- **Tag history** ([#1442](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1442), [#1443](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1443), [#1444](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1444)): Every tag change is recorded and can be restored, and tags changed by other tools are reported after a scan.
- **Collection Curation shelf** ([#1586](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1586), [#1587](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1587), [#1588](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1588)): Find albums without artwork or with missing tracks, and check tag readiness before organizing.
- **Compare and merge duplicates** ([#1678](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1678)) with a preservation-first merge, plus **tag clean-up** of comment clutter ([#1939](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1939)) and a **metadata conflict blocklist** that rejects incorrect auto-matches ([#1581](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1581)).
- **Disc subtitles** for multi-disc releases and box sets ([#1940](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1940)).

### 🏷️ Tags, Genres & Smart Playlists
- **Genre aliases** ([#1942](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1942), [#1994](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1994), [#1995](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1995)): Non-destructively treat spellings as one genre, in headers, search and smart playlists.
- **Tempo and key detection** ([#1943](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1943)) for songs missing those tags, a **Tempo rule** for smart playlists and search ([#2019](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/2019)), and technical and range predicates such as format, lossless, units and ranges ([#1941](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1941)).
- **Country** ([#2095](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/2095), [#2116](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/2116), [#2135](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/2135)): A country field on artist profiles, `country:` search and country auto-playlists, with near-duplicate artist tag spellings offered for merging ([#2096](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/2096)).
- **Language and auto-playlist controls** ([#1084](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1084), [#2131](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/2131)): Browse by language tag and switch auto-playlist families on or off in **Settings → General**.

### 🎧 Playback & Audio
- **Playback speed control** ([#2142](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/2142)).
- **Bit-perfect output** (Epic [#1728](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1728)): WASAPI exclusive mode with device selection ([#1298](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1298)), direct ALSA hardware output on Linux ([#1727](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1727)) that asks PipeWire to release a busy card ([#1844](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1844)) and returns when an unplugged device comes back ([#1877](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1877)), native sample-rate switching ([#1838](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1838)), hardware volume control ([#1847](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1847), [#1891](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1891)) and a volume safety ceiling for high-gain DACs ([#1890](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1890)).
- **Equalizer follows your device** ([#2125](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/2125)): Equalizer state is remembered per playback device.
- **Last.fm** ([#1954](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1954)): Scrobbling and loved-song sync. Last.fm requires a Pro subscription for API keys from 1 December 2026, so Luminous no longer ships a shared key: enter your own API key and shared secret in Settings > Integrations to connect ([#2165](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/2165)).

### 🎵 Library & Organization
- **A-Z jump scrubber** ([#1973](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1973)) on sorted views, and **drag songs to the Queue or a playlist** ([#1974](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1974), [#1972](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1972)).
- **Share Card artist logo** ([#2146](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/2146)): Swap the artist name for their logo.

### 🛠️ Fixes
- **Library**: Albums with the same name by different artists are no longer merged ([#1932](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1932)); case-only duplicates clear on refresh ([#1987](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1987)); two scans can no longer overlap ([#1873](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1873)); relocating a folder on Windows keeps paths and playlists intact ([#1865](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1865)); ALAC `.m4a` and Opus `.ogg` import correctly ([#1866](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1866)); the Favourites count matches ([#1960](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1960)); large libraries stay responsive at startup and large playlists open fast ([#1968](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1968), [#1898](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1898), [#2030](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/2030)); background analysis now waits for the library to settle and pauses during scans, so a forced rescan on a slow drive is quick again ([#2160](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/2160)); the device list refreshes when a library folder changes ([#2161](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/2161)).
- **Integrity**: The corrupt-file check now stores its results, so Curation can show genuinely damaged files ([#2160](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/2160)); a file on a drive that is asleep or unplugged is no longer flagged as corrupt ([#2162](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/2162)).
- **Playback**: Resume honours the fade-in ([#1718](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1718)); ASIO output no longer stalls on song change ([#1858](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1858)).
- **Auto-playlists**: BPM playlists no longer overlap, and a Very Slow bucket covers songs under 60 BPM ([#2017](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/2017)); one language is no longer split across several cards ([#2132](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/2132), [#2136](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/2136)); cards open the right playlist ([#2129](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/2129)); leftover bare-genre playlists are cleaned up ([#2157](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/2157)); the Moment Mix now follows your local time on Windows time zones Luminous didn't recognize, such as British Columbia ([#2169](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/2169)).
- **Artists**: Tags written by other tools are preserved ([#2094](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/2094)), and artist profile names are case-insensitive ([#2097](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/2097)).
- **Interface**: Info panel opens on Information ([#2039](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/2039)); Settings and Help stay visible in short windows ([#2040](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/2040)); context menus scroll and stay on screen ([#2102](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/2102)); consistent hover zoom ([#2103](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/2103)) and pinned tiles ([#2072](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/2072), [#2076](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/2076)); the A-Z scrubber lines up with the list ([#2105](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/2105), [#1992](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1992)); long titles expand in detail headers ([#2121](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/2121)); Top albums week range follows your language ([#2087](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/2087)); no more header flicker with an open overflow menu ([#2089](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/2089)); album tag edits no longer revert while saving ([#1969](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1969)); share cards are centred ([#1956](https://github.com/esoltys/luminous/issues/1956)).
